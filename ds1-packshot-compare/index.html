The fix

  • Both flankers are the same bottle at 94% scale — identical height, identical base line.
  • Tilt is kept and mirrored: left −7°, right +7°. Equal angle, opposite direction.
  • Equal ±163px offset from centre. Left and right margins land within 0px.
  • All three labels read forward. FREE seal unchanged, same position.
